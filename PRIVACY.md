# QR Keyring privacy policy

*Last updated: 25 November 2026*

> In short: QR Keyring has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and doesn't use the internet. It doesn't collect or share any data. Your codes stay on your phone.



## Who is responsible

The QR Keyring app (Llavero QR in Spanish; package app.qrea) is developed by Manuel Arjona. For any privacy question, write to [arjona.apps@gmail.com](mailto:arjona.apps@gmail.com?subject=APP%20Llavero%20QR%20-%20Privacy).



## What data the app uses and where it's stored

QR Keyring works without sign-up. The codes you create or scan, their content, name, category, tags, notes, design and dates, your categories and your settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else. The app has no internet permission and Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off, so nothing leaves that way either.



## Camera

The app asks for camera permission only to scan codes when you open the scanner. The image is analysed live on your phone to find the code; no photos are taken, no images are stored and nothing is sent.



## Images from your gallery

To scan codes from screenshots or photos, or to put a logo in the centre of a code, the app only accesses the images you pick with the system picker or share with it. It can't see the rest of your gallery and doesn't ask for storage permissions. Images used for scanning are analysed on the phone and not stored; logos are copied, downsized, inside the app.



## Sharing, gallery and printing

When you share a code, save it to the gallery or print it, the app creates an image of the code and hands it to the app or service you choose. From then on, that app's or service's privacy policy applies.



## Backups

The backups you export are files saved wherever you decide (for example, your Drive or your phone). The app never sends them on its own.



## Contact emails

If you write to the developer from the app, your email app opens with a message that includes the app and Android versions and the phone model, to help fix the problem. It's only sent if you send it, and that email is used solely to reply to you.



## Children

QR Keyring is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't collect personal data from anyone.



## Your rights

Since the developer doesn't hold your data, there's nothing to access, correct or delete on any server. To remove everything the app stores, clear its data in Android settings or uninstall it. If you write by email, you can ask for that email to be deleted.



## Changes

If this policy changes, it will be published here with its new date.
