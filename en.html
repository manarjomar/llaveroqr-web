All your QR codes, always at hand

Create QR codes with your own design, scan any code with the camera or from a photo, and keep them organised to show or share whenever you need them. Free, no ads, no accounts, and nothing ever leaves your phone.

Coming soon to Google PlayContact

What you can do

🧩 13 code types

Website, Wi‑Fi, contact, phone, SMS, WhatsApp, email, location, event, SEPA transfer, social media, text and barcodes (EAN, Code 128, Data Matrix…).

🎨 Your own design

Any colour, rounded, round, diamond or fluid dots, matching corners and your logo in the centre. A meter warns you when contrast is too low.

📷 Scan any code

With the camera or from saved screenshots and photos, several at once and several codes per image. Inverted and coloured codes too.

🗂️ Neatly organised

Categories with colour and icon, tags, notes, favourites and a search that finds everything. With capture and last-modified dates.

📲 At hand when you need it

Full screen at maximum brightness, share as an image, save to gallery, print, and a one-tap action: join the Wi‑Fi, add the contact, open the website…

💾 Backup

Export all your codes and categories to a file and import them on another phone without duplicates.

Your privacy

QR Keyring has no accounts, no ads, no servers and doesn't use the internet. Your codes stay on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
